1. About the store

Java Pet Store Lite is an online pet catalog. You can browse pets by category, search the catalog, create an account, sign in and review your recent sign-in activity. The store has three functions:

FunctionWhat you can doSign-in needed
Catalog browsing and searchBrowse categories, products and items. Search by keyword.No
Sign-on and accountCreate an account, sign in, view your account and sign-in history, sign out.For My Account
Sample dataThe catalog is loaded automatically the first time the store is opened.No

The store is in English (en_US) and shows prices in US dollars.

1.1 Page layout

Every page has the same four parts:

  • Banner at the top: the Java Pet Store logo (returns to the home page), the Search box, and the links Account and Sign in (or Sign out when you are signed in). When you are signed in the banner shows Signed in as <user name>.
  • Pets side bar on the left: one link per category, in alphabetical order.
  • Body in the middle: the page you asked for.
  • Footer at the bottom.

2. Entering the store

  1. Open http://<server>:8080/petstore/. The entry page appears.
  2. Click enter the store. If this is the first visit after installation, the store loads its catalog first. A red message Please wait while the Java Pet Store Application is being populated... shows while it loads.
  3. The home page opens with a picture of the pets. Click a pet in the picture, or a category in the side bar.

3. Browsing the catalog

The catalog has three levels:

LevelExamplePage
CategoryDogsLists the products in the category
ProductBulldog (K9-BD-01)Lists the items of the product with their list price
ItemMale Adult Bulldog (EST-6)Shows the picture, List Price, Your Price, description and Item ID

The store has five categories: Birds, Cats, Dogs, Fish and Reptiles.

3.1 Browse a category

  1. Click a category in the Pets side bar, for example Dogs.
  2. The page Products for this Category lists two products at a time, each with its name and description.
  3. Click Next to see the next two products, or Previous to go back. A link only appears when there is a page in that direction.

3.2 View the items of a product

  1. Click a product name, for example Bulldog.
  2. The page Items for this Product lists the items, two at a time, with the item attribute, name, description and list price.

3.3 View an item

Click an item. The item page shows:

  • List Price: the catalog price.
  • Your Price: the discounted price for members.
  • The description and the Item ID (for example EST-6). Quote the Item ID when you contact the store.

4. Searching

  1. Type one or more words in the Search box in the banner and click Search.
  2. The page Search Results lists the matching items, two at a time, with Next and Previous links.
  3. Click an item to open its item page.

How search matches:

  • A word matches when it appears anywhere in the product name, the category code (for example DOGS) or the item description. Part of a word is enough: dog finds Bulldog and every item in the DOGS category.
  • When you type several words separated by spaces, items that match any of the words are listed.
  • Search is not case-sensitive.
  • If nothing matches, or the box is empty, the page shows No results were found for your search.

5. Your account

5.1 Create an account

  1. Click Sign in in the banner, or click Account. The Sign In page appears.
  2. In the right-hand box No. I would like to sign up for an account, enter a User Name, a Password and the password again in Password (Repeat).
  3. Click Create New Account. You are signed in at once and taken to the page you asked for, or to the home page.
FieldRuleMessage when the rule is broken
User Name3 to 25 charactersThe user name must be 3 to 25 characters.
User NameLetters, digits, '-' and '_' onlyThe user name can only contain letters, digits, '-' and '_'.
User NameMust not already existThe user name <name> is already taken. Choose another user name.
Password4 to 25 charactersThe password must be 4 to 25 characters.
Password (Repeat)Must equal PasswordThe password and the confirmation do not match.

User names are case-sensitive: J2EE and j2ee are different users. New accounts are customer accounts.

5.2 Sign in

  1. Click Sign in. In the left-hand box Yes., enter your User Name and Password.
  2. Tick Remember My User Name if you want the store to fill in your user name next time. The store keeps it in a browser cookie for 31 days. Untick it to forget the name.
  3. Click Sign In. You return to the page you were trying to open, or to the home page.

If the user name or password is wrong the page Sign-in Error appears. Click try again.

5.3 Locked account

After 3 failed sign-in attempts in a row your account is locked. While it is locked you cannot sign in, even with the right password.

The page Account Locked appears. The lock is cleared automatically 30 minutes after it was set (the store checks every 15 minutes, so allow up to 45 minutes). You can keep browsing the catalog while you wait. A successful sign-in resets the count of failed attempts to zero.

5.4 My Account

Click Account in the banner. If you are not signed in, the Sign In page appears first. My Account shows your user name, your role and your 10 most recent sign-in events, newest first.

EventMeaning
USER_CREATEDThe account was created
SIGNON_OKSuccessful sign-in
SIGNON_FAILWrong password (or unknown user name)
ACCOUNT_LOCKEDThe third failure in a row locked the account
SIGNON_LOCKEDA sign-in was tried while the account was locked
ACCOUNT_UNLOCKEDThe lock was cleared by the store
SIGNOFFYou signed out

If you see events you do not recognise, change nothing and contact the store administrator.

5.5 Sign out

Click Sign out in the banner. The page You are signed out appears. If you leave the store without signing out, your session ends after 15 minutes without activity.
